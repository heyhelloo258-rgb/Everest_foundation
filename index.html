Who We Are

Everest Foundation is a community-focused charitable initiative dedicated to improving lives and creating opportunities for people in need.


Our goal is to build a society where every child gets an opportunity to learn, every senior citizen receives dignity and care, every person with a disability gets support, and every family can access essential assistance.


Through community service, educational support, healthcare assistance, and cultural activities, we work towards creating a more compassionate and inclusive society.
